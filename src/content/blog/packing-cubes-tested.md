## The premise

Most "compression" cubes shave a centimetre. The right cubes shave half a bag.

## The winners

A small set of three: a flat folder for shirts, a compression cube for knitwear, and a wash bag with a hook. Everything else is marketing.

Skip: anything with the word "expandable" on the listing. The zips fail on the third trip and the seams give up by month six.
