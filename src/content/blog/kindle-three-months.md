## Which Kindle, and why

Three months. Three Kindles. One overnight flight where the Scribe quietly earned its place. The Paperwhite remains the best buy for most readers — bigger screen, warm-light tuning, no premium you'll never use.

## What the Scribe is for

It's a notebook substitute, not a notebook replacement. Latency is fine, the eraser works as expected, and the export-to-email flow is the one that ended up sticking. If you're a heavy long-form annotator, the Scribe earns its keep. If you're not, the Paperwhite is the buy.

## Where the Colorsoft falls down

The colour is muted, the refresh is slower than the Paperwhite, and the warm light tuning is regressed. Wait a generation or two.
